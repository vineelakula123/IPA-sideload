How Computer-Free iPhone IPA Sideloading Works

iOS installs apps outside the App Store without a computer using Apple’s Over-The-Air deployment protocol (itms-services://?action=download-manifest&url=...). For iOS to accept and launch the app on your Home Screen, two technical requirements must be met:

1. Valid Code Signature (.p12 + .mobileprovision)

Every iOS executable (Payload/AppName.app/AppName) must have a cryptographic CMS signature matching a certificate (.p12) and provisioning profile (.mobileprovision) that includes your iPhone’s UDID (Ad-Hoc/Development) or an Enterprise certificate.

2. HTTPS Hosting for manifest.plist & .ipa

When you tap Install on iPhone (OTA) in Safari, iOS itunesstored fetches the manifest.plist over trusted HTTPS (it rejects local blob: or plain http:// URLs). Host your signed .ipa and generated manifest.plist on any free HTTPS bucket (GitHub Releases, Cloudflare R2, or a self-hosted web signer) and paste the URL in Step 2.

3. Enable Developer Mode / Trust Certificate on iPhone

On iOS 16–26, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode (for Developer/Ad-Hoc certs) or Settings → General → VPN & Device Management to trust the enterprise profile.
